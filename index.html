Ensuring Safe, Compliant, and Drift-Free AI in Healthcare

CuraOps AI is an open-source, synthetic-data-driven MLOps QA framework. We enable hospitals and health-tech teams to validate, monitor, and deploy clinical AI models with zero PHI exposure.

Explore the Framework See How It Works

The Healthcare AI Bottleneck

Hospitals want to deploy AI, but critical roadblocks prevent safe adoption.

HIPAA & Privacy Risks

Testing AI models on real patient data violates HIPAA and exposes institutions to massive legal liability.

Silent Model Drift

AI accuracy degrades over time as patient demographics change, leading to dangerous clinical hallucinations.

API Interoperability

Legacy EHR systems and modern AI endpoints frequently fail to communicate, causing critical data pipeline breaks.

The CuraOps AI Solution

A standardized, privacy-first testing pipeline that allows health-tech teams to validate clinical AI models using 100% synthetic data.

🛡️ Synthetic-First Architecture

Generates realistic, de-identified patient histories using Synthea™. Guarantees zero PHI exposure during the entire QA phase.

🩺 Clinical Safety Gates

Pre-configured Great Expectations validation suites enforce medical boundary checks (e.g., heart rate 40-180 bpm) before data hits the model.

Automated MLOps Monitoring

Integrates MLflow and automated pytest suites to detect model drift, validate API latency (<200ms), and ensure continuous reliability.

National Importance & Impact

Solving a Critical U.S. Healthcare Infrastructure Gap

CuraOps AI directly addresses the "fundamental lack of engineering approach" to synthetic data identified in recent peer-reviewed literature (Pileggi & Liu, 2026). By providing an open-source, pre-production QA framework, we accelerate safe AI adoption, reduce clinical risk, and ensure strict HIPAA compliance for hospitals nationwide.
